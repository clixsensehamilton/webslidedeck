# Script Template — AI/ML Infographic Series

Each post is one scroll-page infographic. Use this template for every script file.

---

## File Naming Convention
`day-[NN]-[mon|tue|wed|thu|fri]-[short-slug].md`
Example: `day-01-mon-the-question-every-it-pro-should-be-asking.md`

---

## Script Format

```markdown
---
week: [1-13]

weekday: [Monday|Tuesday|Wednesday|Thursday|Friday]
chapter: [Chapter title]
source_video: [Video N: Title]
post_title: [Exact title from V3 lineup]
series_tag: "#AIMLSeries #Module1"
---

## Hook
[1-2 punchy sentences. This is what appears first as the user enters the scroll page.

[First scroll reveal. Sets up the core concept. 2-4 sentences max.

## Scene 2 — [Scene Title]
[Second scroll reveal. The mechanism or explanation. 2-4 sentences max.

## Scene 3 — [Scene Title]
[Third scroll reveal. A concrete example, analogy, or case. 2-4 sentences max.
Ground the concept in something real and recognisable.]

## Scene 4 — [Scene Title] (optional)
[Fourth scroll reveal. Deeper nuance, a comparison, or a surprising implication. 2-4 sentences max.

[One sentence. Bold and punchy. What the reader walks away knowing or thinking.
```

---

## Tone Guidelines
- **Confident, not condescending.** The audience manages systems, knows how networks work, has debugged production issues. Write for them.
- **Technical-adjacent, not academic.** Use precise language but don't lead with jargon — lead with the consequence or the problem, then name the term.
- **Original content only.** Do not copy or closely paraphrase source material. The source informs the concept; the writing is original.
- **No buzzword soup.** Every sentence should say something specific. "AI is transforming industries" is a wasted sentence.
- **IT angle where available.** If the concept maps to something IT professionals already do (monitoring, access control, pipeline ownership, change management), make that connection explicit.

---

## Scene Count Guide
- **Fridays ("What This Means for IT"):** 3 scenes max — these are synthesis posts, not teaching posts
- **Conceptual intro posts (Mondays):** 3-4 scenes — set up the week's frame
- **Deep-dive posts (Tue-Thu):** 3-4 scenes — go one layer deeper than the title
- **Case study posts:** 3 scenes — problem, what the model did, result
