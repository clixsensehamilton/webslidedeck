## Hook
Thirteen weeks ago, AI was a word your organisation used in strategy decks. Now you know what's behind it.

## Scene 1 — How the Journey Started
The module opened with a question about why AI is happening now — and the answer was a convergence: data infrastructure, computational power, and algorithmic maturity arriving at the same moment. That wasn't a marketing premise. It was the structural explanation for why organisations that ignored AI five years ago can't afford to ignore it today. Understanding that convergence is what separates a professional who can engage with AI decisions from one who can only observe them.

## Scene 2 — What the Middle of the Journey Built
From that foundation, the module moved through what was actually constructed: the machine learning paradigm that replaced brittle rule systems with systems that learn. The taxonomy of how machines learn — supervised, unsupervised, semi-supervised, reinforcement — each with a distinct mechanism and a distinct set of appropriate problems. The failure modes that emerge when data is poor, when models overfit, when the training set doesn't represent the population it's predicting for. Each chapter built on the one before it because each failure mode is only legible if you understand what the system was trying to do first.

## Scene 3 — What the End of the Journey Demanded
The module closed not with a success story but with a reckoning. Real-world applications produced real wins — efficiency, personalisation, fraud reduction. They also produced bias, opacity, and privacy risk. Addressing those risks requires the same competence as addressing any other infrastructure problem: understanding the system, knowing where it can fail, and building the governance that keeps it accountable. That's the arc — from foundational concept to deployed capability to the responsibility that capability creates.

## Scene 4 — What You're Leaving With
You are now equipped with a foundational understanding of how AI and ML systems are built, how they learn, where they fail, and what responsible deployment looks like. That's not a certificate — it's a working vocabulary and a set of mental models that apply to any AI system you encounter, evaluate, or are asked to support. The module gave you the concepts. The questions it equipped you to ask are yours to use.

## Takeaway
The distance from "AI is transforming industries" to understanding how, why, where it fails, and what it costs when it does — that's exactly the distance this module covered.
