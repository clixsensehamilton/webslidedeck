## Hook
Last week we saw AI cut account takeovers by 40% and save a sales team $2.5 million. Those numbers are real. So is everything that comes next.

## Scene 1 — The Same Technology, a Different Story
AI has been making measurable gains across healthcare, finance, and retail. The progress is genuine. But the same systems producing those results also carry a shadow that doesn't show up in the wins column. Bias, opacity, discrimination, and privacy exposure are not edge cases — they are documented, recurring failure modes built into how these systems are trained and deployed.

## Scene 2 — Where the Problems Come From
These aren't software bugs you can patch in a hotfix. They emerge from the data AI is trained on, the decisions it's asked to make autonomously, and the environments it operates in — environments with regulatory obligations, civil rights implications, and real human consequences. The model does what the data and the design reward. If the data is flawed or the design ignores fairness, the output reflects that every time it runs.

## Scene 3 — Why This Week Exists in This Module
Weeks 9 and 10 covered what happens when ML fails technically — overfitting, underfitting, bad data. This week covers what happens when ML fails ethically. Those aren't separate problems. An AI system running correctly on biased training data is not a failing system by most technical metrics. It's a system performing exactly as designed — and that's precisely the issue.

## Scene 4 — The Stakes for IT
IT professionals are increasingly the people who build, maintain, and sign off on these systems. Understanding ethical failure modes isn't a compliance checkbox — it's operational knowledge. When an AI-assisted decision gets challenged in a regulated environment, someone in the chain is accountable. This week is about understanding what you're accountable for.

## Takeaway
The wins from Week 11 are real. So are the risks from Week 12 — and the difference between them is whether the people building and running these systems understand both.
