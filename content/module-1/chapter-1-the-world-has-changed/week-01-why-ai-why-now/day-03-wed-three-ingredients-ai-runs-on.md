## Hook
The Venn diagram from yesterday isn't just an academic framework. Map it against what IT actually does day-to-day, and something becomes clear: IT has been living inside the data science stack for years without calling it that.

## Scene 1 — The Ingredient IT Knows Best: Computer Science
The computer science pillar of data science isn't just coding — it covers data engineering, systems infrastructure, pipeline construction, and the wrangling that turns raw inputs into something a model can actually use. IT teams own storage architecture, network throughput, data access controls, and the reliability of the systems that move data from source to destination. Every one of those responsibilities sits squarely inside the CS pillar.

## Scene 2 — The Ingredient IT Influences: Domain Knowledge
Domain knowledge sounds like it belongs to the business side — and most of it does. But IT professionals who've worked in healthcare, finance, manufacturing, or government bring operational context that data teams frequently lack. You know which data is trustworthy, which systems are maintained, where the gaps are, and what "this output doesn't look right" actually means in practice. That's not zero domain knowledge. That's earned signal.

## Scene 3 — The Ingredient IT Enables: Math and Statistics
IT doesn't typically own the algorithm layer — that's the data science team's domain. But IT enables it. The compute resources that run training jobs, the GPU clusters that power inference, the monitoring systems that flag when model outputs drift outside expected ranges — these are IT's responsibility. You may not write the equations, but you keep the machines that solve them running.

## Takeaway
IT doesn't sit outside the data science stack waiting to be handed requirements — it holds the infrastructure that makes the whole stack possible.

## Visual Direction
Return to the three-circle Venn diagram from Tuesday. This time, highlight each circle with IT-specific annotations: "Computer Science" circle annotates with pipeline, storage, access control; "Domain Knowledge" circle annotates with operational context, system reliability knowledge; "Math & Statistics" circle annotates with compute, GPU, monitoring. The centre "Data Science" label remains, now surrounded by IT's fingerprints on all three circles. The animation should feel like revealing what was always there — not adding anything new.
